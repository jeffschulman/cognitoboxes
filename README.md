# cognitoboxes.sty

🎨 A set of LaTeX environments for thematic callouts—designed for metamodern, cybersecurity, and philosophical writing.

## Environments Included
- `infoshiftbox` 💡 – Highlight a critical inflection or shift in perspective.
- `conceptbox` 🧠 – Mark a conceptual framework or theoretical pivot.
- `ethicsbox` ⚖️ – Flag ethical or moral considerations.
- `warningbox` ⚠️ – Warn of systemic risks, gaps, or critical vulnerabilities.
- `futurebox` 🌌 – Signal visionary, speculative, or horizon-thinking remarks.
- `metabox` ♾️ – Use for recursive, self-referential, or epistemological insights.

## Usage

Place `cognitoboxes.sty` in your working directory and include in your preamble:

```latex
\usepackage{cognitoboxes}
```

Then use boxes like:

```latex
\begin{infoshiftbox}
Information is immediately accessible and globally transmissible.
\end{infoshiftbox}
```
