\section*{Overview}

This document demonstrates the usage of the six themed callout boxes provided by the \texttt{cognitoboxes.sty} LaTeX package.

\begin{infoshiftbox}
Information is immediately accessible, globally transmissible, and trivially duplicable in the digital age.
\end{infoshiftbox}

\begin{conceptbox}
The Free Energy Principle reframes security not as detection but as prediction and adaptive coherence.
\end{conceptbox}

\begin{ethicsbox}
Security policies must include considerations of human psychological load, not just system resilience.
\end{ethicsbox}

\begin{warningbox}
Overclassification can hinder coordination, obscure threats, and result in catastrophic oversights.
\end{warningbox}

\begin{futurebox}
In the Cognitocene, safeguarding information means safeguarding systems of meaning and agency.
\end{futurebox}

\begin{metabox}

\end{metabox}
